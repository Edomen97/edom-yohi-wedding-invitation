Our Love Story

የፍቅራችን ታሪክ

Yohi, you are not just someone I chose to walk beside. You are the woman who makes my future feel beautiful and my heart feel at home. Your smile is a blessing in my life, your presence is my happiness, and your place in my heart is one that nobody else could ever take.

I do not promise a perfect life, but I promise to stand beside you, respect you, protect our dreams, pray with you, and choose you through every season of life.

You are my Yohi, my special person, my future wife, and the woman with whom I want to build a beautiful family.

— Edom

ዮሂዬ፣ አንቺ በሕይወቴ ውስጥ የመጣሽ ተራ ሰው አይደለሽም። ለወደፊት ሕይወቴ ውበትን፣ ለልቤም የእረፍት ቦታን የሰጠሽኝ ልዩ ሰው ነሽ። ፈገግታሽ የደስታዬ ምንጭ፣ መኖርሽ ደግሞ በሕይወቴ ያለ በረከት ነው።

ፍጹም ሕይወት እንደምሰጥሽ ቃል አልገባም፤ ነገር ግን በደስታም በፈተናም ከጎንሽ እንደምቆም፣ እንደማከብርሽ፣ ሕልማችንን አብረን እንደምንገነባ፣ በጸሎትም እንደምንተባበር ቃል እገባለሁ።

ዮሂዬ፣ ለእኔ ልዩ ነሽ። ከአንቺ ጋር ቤተሰብ መመሥረትና የሕይወት ጉዟችንን አብረን መቀጠል የልቤ ትልቁ ምኞት ነው።

— ኤዶም

To Our Family & Friends

ለቤተሰቦቻችንና ለወዳጆቻችን

Dear Family and Friends,

Your presence is one of the greatest gifts we could receive. Thank you for celebrating this special chapter of our lives with us. May the joy you share with us today remain in your hearts for years to come.

May God bless you, guide your journeys, and fill your homes with peace and happiness.

With love and gratitude,
Edom & Yohi
